Registration Growth
March daily average +34% vs February (869 vs 647). Peak week Mar 7–14 averaged 1,005/day — sustained marketing campaign effect. Feb 14 Valentine's Day spike: 923 registrations (+43% above Feb avg).
iOS Dominance
App Store drives 83.1% of CDP events with highest engagement depth (24.4 events/user vs 14.2 for Android). iOS represents 87.1% of all platform events. Android (~30% of US smartphone market) is underpenetrated at 12.7% — growth opportunity.
Feb 22–23 Anomaly
Registration count dropped from 365 → 66 on Feb 23 (89% single-day collapse). Surrounding days averaged 600+. Likely a data pipeline issue, not genuine traffic drop. Recommend verifying with registration system logs.
