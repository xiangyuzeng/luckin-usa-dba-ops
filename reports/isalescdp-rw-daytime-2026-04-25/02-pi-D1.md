## Recommendation
Enable PI on this instance (free for 7-day retention, $0.012/vCPU/h for long retention). Without PI, every future incident on this host requires the slow-log/perf_schema fallback above, which is much coarser and has a 0.1s threshold blind spot for sub-100ms hot queries.
